Priest Class Redesign

A systems-driven class redesign project focused on preserving Priest identity while improving combat cadence, tuning clarity, resource interaction, and moment-to-moment decision making.

Overview

Two redesigns, one assessment, one shared goal.

This project explores how Shadow and Discipline Priest could be modernized without losing the ideas that make each spec recognizable. Holy is assessed separately. Its foundation is sound, and its problems are tuning-level rather than structural. Not every spec needs to be rebuilt.

Shadow

Pressure conversion

Shadow builds pressure through DoTs and Resonance, then converts that setup into priority damage through Voidform windows and payoff abilities.

Discipline

Damage shaping

Discipline no longer directly converts damage into healing. Instead, Atonement delays damage, Contrition stores healing potential, and release spells resolve pressure.

Holy

Finish, don't rebuild

The Holy Word system works. The fantasy is intact. The problem is a strong burst window around Apotheosis and a thin baseline between them: a cooldown pacing and mana floor issue, not a structural one.

Design Philosophy

The redesigns focus on identity, constraints, and tunability.

Design Focus Shadow Discipline Holy
Fantasy Preservation Shadowform remains the default stance and visual identity. Discipline remains an offensive healer, not a generic reactive healer. Holy remains a pure healer with clear spell-to-moment mapping. The Holy Word system is preserved as-is.
Problem Identified Passive damage duplication and volatile multi-target scaling. Direct damage-to-healing conversion and shield dominance. A strong Apotheosis burst window and a thin baseline between them. Mana economy swings with the cooldown cycle. Tuning and pacing problems. Not structural ones.
Design Response Full redesign. New core system required. Full redesign. New healing model required. Assessment only. The foundation works. Raise the mana floor between Apotheosis windows, reduce Prayer of Healing cost outside Lightweaver, and give the baseline more consequence.
Player Skill Expression Target choice, Resonance timing, Voidform planning, and payoff execution. Atonement coverage, Contrition buildup, release timing, and mana sustain. Holy Word timing, Apotheosis planning, Prayer of Mending stack management, and Lightweaver setup. The skill expression exists. It needs a stronger baseline to matter between windows.
Tuning Constraint Flat damage, caps, target limits, and controlled payoff windows. Deferred Damage cap, Contrition stack limits, and spell-specific mana returns. Apotheosis cooldown pacing, Prayer of Healing base cost, and mana floor between burst windows.
Project Scope

What this design resource is meant to show.

Included

  • Problem framing and design goals
  • Core gameplay loop and system proposals
  • Spell and kit treatment
  • Tradeoffs and failure cases
  • Priority lists and sample timelines
  • Tooltip-style player-facing language
  • Spec assessment when redesign is not warranted

Not included

  • Final numerical tuning
  • Complete talent tree layouts
  • Encounter-by-encounter balance sheets
  • Full simulation or log validation
  • Production UI mockups beyond icon/state examples
Design takeaway: This project is intended to organize modern Priest redesign ideas with clear systems reasoning, constraint awareness, and player-facing clarity. It is not a final expansion-ready balance proposal. Recognizing when a spec needs a structural overhaul, and when it does not, is part of the design work.

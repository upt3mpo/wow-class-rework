Detailed Spec Design

Discipline System Rules

This page defines the system rules behind Deferred Atonement, Contrition, Siphoned Faith, healing releases, talent direction, UI requirements, tuning levers, encounter examples, and how Discipline handles triage, group damage, rot, burst, Mythic+, and raid healing without direct damage-to-healing conversion.

Design target: Discipline remains an offensive healer, but damage no longer directly converts into healing. Damage builds healing potential, sustains mana, and prepares the priest to resolve incoming pressure through deliberate release spells.
Talent target: Choice nodes should lean Discipline toward a healing profile, not create a pass-or-fail build check. A dungeon-focused player should still answer boss damage. A raid-focused player should still answer triage. Talents change comfort, cadence, and efficiency, not baseline access to required healing patterns.
Design Summary

The detailed systems preserve the overview’s core thesis: Discipline shapes damage, prepares healing, and releases pressure deliberately.

Problem

Direct Atonement healing makes Discipline difficult to tune. If damage is strong, healing becomes too strong. If healing is controlled, damage often feels weak or purely functional.

Goal

Preserve offensive healer identity while separating damage, healing, and mana tuning so each system can be adjusted without breaking the others.

Solution

Atonement delays a capped amount of incoming damage. Offensive spells build Contrition and restore mana. Healing spells release Contrition to heal and clear Deferred Damage.

Resulting Playstyle

Discipline applies Atonement, attacks to prepare allies, smooths incoming damage, and chooses when to resolve pressure with Penance, Radiance, Prayer of Healing, Shield, or cooldowns.

System contract: Atonement enables the system. Offense builds the resource. Incoming damage creates pressure. Healing releases resolve that pressure.
System Relationship

The core loop is simple, but each state has strict rules.

Atonement Marks allies and enables capped damage shaping.
Contrition Stores healing potential through offense.
Release Consumes stacks to heal and clear pressure.
Apply Atonement → Build Contrition → Defer Damage → Release Healing → Sustain Mana
Atonement Rules

Atonement marks allies for damage shaping, not direct healing conversion.

Ally State

Atonement

Atonement enables Discipline’s system on an ally. While active, a capped portion of incoming damage is delayed, and the ally can receive Contrition stacks from the priest’s offensive spells.

Application

How Atonement is applied

Atonement is applied through Power Word: Radiance, Power Word: Shield, Flash Heal, Shadow Mend, Plea, and selected cooldown interactions such as Evangelism.

Baseline duration

Atonement lasts 15 sec from normal applicators. Power Word: Radiance applies a shorter 12 sec Atonement to affected allies to keep raid and dungeon setup windows tunable.

Applicator intent

Shield and direct heals are precise applicators. Radiance is the group applicator. Evangelism is the planned-window extender. The player should choose an applicator based on expected damage, not because one spell is always mathematically required.

Applicator Atonement Role Design Intent
Power Word: Shield Precise single-target application Used for predictable danger, tank pressure, or emergency mitigation. Strong, but too expensive to become filler.
Flash Heal / Shadow Mend Reactive single-target application Gives Discipline a direct recovery path when setup was missed or a single ally suddenly becomes unsafe.
Plea Low-intensity maintenance application Useful for light preparation or downtime without competing with Shield as premium mitigation.
Power Word: Radiance Group application and group release Prepares multiple allies and can also consume Contrition to resolve group pressure.
Evangelism Window extension and preparation accelerator Supports planned raid events without turning every moment into a scripted ramp requirement.
Rule change: Atonement no longer says “your damage heals this ally.” Instead, it says “this ally is prepared for Discipline’s damage-shaping, Contrition, and release systems.”
Tuning levers: Atonement duration, Radiance duration, applicator mana cost, number of Radiance targets, Evangelism extension amount, and which cooldowns can seed initial Contrition.
Deferred Damage

Discipline delays damage instead of absorbing it.

This preserves the controlled, preventive feeling of shield-heavy Discipline without allowing shields to erase damage outright.

Damage Shaping

Deferred Damage

Allies with Atonement have 25% of incoming damage delayed over 6 sec instead of taken immediately. This delayed damage is visible on the party frame as Deferred Damage.

Deferred cap

Deferred Damage cannot exceed 25% of the target’s maximum health. If the pool is capped, remaining damage is taken immediately. This prevents infinite safety and naturally scales the buffer with the player’s gear.

Atonement Active The ally is eligible for damage delay.
Damage Deferred A capped portion of damage is delayed over time.
Pressure Resolved Release spells heal and clear Deferred Damage.
Rule Value Reason
Damage delayed 25% of incoming damage Meaningful enough to smooth pressure without replacing healer response.
Delay duration 6 sec Long enough for the priest to react, short enough that ignored pressure remains dangerous.
Pool cap 25% of target maximum health Prevents stacked effects from creating functional immunity.
Overflow behavior Excess damage is taken immediately Large hits still threaten the target if the deferred pool is already full.
Release interaction Healing releases reduce or clear the pool Turns healing choice into pressure resolution, not passive prevention.
Design intent: Deferred Damage gives the healer time to respond without removing danger. It smooths pressure, but it does not make allies safe by default.
Tuning levers: Percent delayed, delay duration, maximum health cap, overflow behavior, and how much Deferred Damage is cleared per Contrition stack.
Contrition Rules

Contrition turns offensive uptime into stored release potential.

Ally Resource

How Contrition builds

Offensive spells generate Contrition on allies affected by Atonement. Contrition stacks up to 5 times per ally and lasts 12 sec. Adding a new stack refreshes the duration.

Release

How Contrition is consumed

Penance, Power Word: Radiance, Prayer of Healing, and selected cooldowns consume Contrition to heal allies and reduce their Deferred Damage pool.

Builder spells

Smite, Mind Blast, offensive Penance, Shadow Word: Pain ticks, Shadow Word: Death, Entropic Rift, and Voidweaver spells can build Contrition while Atonement is active.

Release logic

Single-target releases consume more stacks from one ally. Group releases consume fewer stacks across several allies. This gives tuning control between spot healing, group healing, and Deferred Damage resolution.

Builder Contrition Behavior Intent
Smite Low stack generation Baseline preparation and filler sustain during low pressure.
Mind Blast Medium stack generation Rewards using high-value offensive cooldowns during setup.
Offensive Penance Medium / high stack generation Keeps active offensive-healer gameplay central to the spec.
Shadow Word: Pain Periodic low stack generation Provides background preparation without becoming direct passive healing.
Shadow Word: Death High stack generation Rewards execute timing and riskier offensive windows.
Entropic Rift / Voidweaver spells Windowed enhanced generation Supports aggressive healing windows while keeping healing release separate from damage dealt.
Core distinction: Damage does not heal directly. Damage prepares healing and sustain.
Tuning levers: Contrition stack cap, stack duration, stack generation per spell, per-cast versus periodic generation, target count limits, and whether specific cooldowns seed initial stacks.
Healing Release Rules

Healing spells resolve stored pressure in different patterns.

The release system lets the kit distinguish single-target triage, group recovery, sustained rot stabilization, and major cooldown recovery without making every heal depend on identical setup or identical target counts.

Release Spell Contrition Use Primary Role
Defensive Penance Consumes all stacks from the target Strong triage release for a tank or endangered ally.
Power Word: Radiance Consumes up to 3 stacks from affected allies Group release for planned or sudden party pressure.
Prayer of Healing Consumes 1 stack from each ally healed Efficient sustain release during rot or low-to-medium damage.
Ultimate Penitence Consumes stacks dynamically from injured allies Major cooldown release and stabilization tool.

Base heals remain functional

Contrition improves efficiency and pressure resolution, but it should not make normal healing unusable. Discipline still needs a reactive floor when setup was missed or an ally is unexpectedly targeted.

Overheal conversion is limited

Release overhealing can reduce Deferred Damage on the same target, but should not freely spill into full group healing. This keeps release timing valuable without making it impossible to waste.

Existing Kit Treatment

The redesign changes the healing model, not the entire spellbook.

This section clarifies which tools are retained, reworked, constrained, or used as supporting systems.

Spell / System Treatment Design Reason

Siphoned Faith New mana engine Rewards offensive play with mana sustain so pet or shield paths are not mandatory mana fixes.
Power Word: Shield / Void Shield Retained as premium mitigation Shields remain valuable but should not become the default answer to every damage pattern.
Penance / Power Word: Radiance / Prayer of Healing Retained as release tools These spells resolve stored pressure by consuming Contrition and reducing Deferred Damage.

Mana Economy

Siphoned Faith makes offensive play part of Discipline’s sustain.

Passive

Siphoned Faith

Offensive spells restore mana when they generate Contrition. Higher-impact offensive spells return more mana than filler spells.

Constraint

Shield remains expensive

Power Word: Shield remains a premium mitigation tool. It is not intended to be the default Atonement maintenance spell in every situation.

Spell Mana Return Intent
Smite Small Baseline sustain during downtime.
Mind Blast Medium Rewards using meaningful offensive cooldowns.
Offensive Penance Medium / High Supports active offensive-healer play.
Shadow Word: Death High Rewards execute sniping and high-risk offensive timing.
Entropic Rift / Voidweaver spells Windowed Supports aggressive healing windows without directly copying damage into healing.

Mana should reward uptime, not demand perfection

Offensive play should noticeably extend Discipline’s resources, but missing a few offensive globals should not make the spec collapse. The goal is positive reinforcement, not a failure tax.

Pets remain optional accelerators

Shadowfiend and Mindbender can still support offensive pressure and mana return, but the baseline Siphoned Faith loop prevents pet talents from becoming mandatory sustain fixes.

Priority Lists

Discipline uses healing priorities, not a fixed script.

Baseline Dungeon Priority

Mythic+ healing priority

Maintain Atonement on key allies

Keep Atonement active on the tank and allies likely to take damage. Use Radiance for group setup.

Build Contrition through offense

Use Smite, Mind Blast, offensive Penance, Shadow Word: Pain, and Shadow Word: Death to prepare healing.

Maintain Siphoned Faith uptime

Offensive casts stabilize mana while building Contrition.

Use Penance to resolve priority damage

Defensive Penance consumes Contrition from the target to heal and reduce Deferred Damage.

Use Radiance for group pressure

Radiance applies or refreshes Atonement and releases Contrition across multiple allies.

Use Prayer of Healing for efficient sustain

Use when the group is under sustained pressure but does not need a full Radiance release.

Shield intentionally

Use Shield for predictable danger, tank pressure, or emergency mitigation, not as a default filler.

Raid Priority

Planned damage priority

Prepare with Evangelism

Extend Atonement and seed Contrition before known raid damage.

Build through high-value offense

Use Mind Blast, offensive Penance, Shadow Word: Pain, and Smite to build Contrition before the event.

Let Atonement smooth the hit

Incoming damage is partially deferred, giving the priest time to release stored healing.

Release with Radiance or Prayer

Group releases heal and reduce Deferred Damage across injured allies.

Example Choice Nodes

Talent choices should bias Discipline toward a healing profile without creating trap builds.

The baseline spec already has triage tools, group healing tools, rot management, burst preparation, raid planning, and Mythic+ recovery. These example nodes change how the priest prefers to solve those problems. They should not make the opposite situation feel broken.

Choice Node 1

Triage Release versus Group Release

This node changes the preferred release target. Neither option is a pure single-target or pure AoE lock. Both still use Contrition and Deferred Damage.

Triage Lean

Focused Contrition

Defensive Penance consumes Contrition more efficiently on its target. If Penance consumes 4 or more stacks, it also reduces a small amount of Deferred Damage from the lowest-health nearby Atoned ally.

Design reason:
Stronger tank or priority-target recovery without abandoning the party during dungeon-wide damage.
Group Lean

Shared Contrition

Power Word: Radiance and Prayer of Healing release Contrition from one additional Atoned ally. If fewer than 3 allies are injured, a portion of the release is redirected to the most injured Atoned ally.

Design reason:
Better group smoothing without making the talent dead during a boss phase with only one dangerous target.
Choice Node 2

Rot Stabilization versus Burst Preparation

This node changes timing. One side rewards frequent smaller releases. The other rewards stronger pre-planning before a known event.

Rot Lean

Patient Absolution

Prayer of Healing consumes 1 Contrition from each target healed and causes the next 2 sec of Deferred Damage on those allies to tick 20% slower. This does not increase the Deferred Damage cap.

Design reason:
Sustained pressure becomes easier to stabilize, but the talent does not trivialize burst because it slows damage rather than increasing the total buffer.
Burst Lean

Prepared Absolution

Evangelism grants 1 additional Contrition to affected allies and causes their first release within 8 sec to reduce extra Deferred Damage. This bonus is reduced if fewer than 3 allies are Atoned.

Design reason:
Better raid-style planning without making the talent mandatory for single-target or small-party recovery.
Choice Node 3

Mythic+ Responsiveness versus Raid Coverage

This node changes how Atonement coverage is maintained. One side favors fast dungeon pivots. The other favors planned raid setup.

Mythic+ Lean

Rapid Reprieve

Power Word: Shield and Flash Heal extend Atonement by 2 sec on the target when they are below 50% health. This effect can occur once every 12 sec per ally.

Design reason:
Supports quick dungeon triage and target swaps without becoming the best raid-wide Atonement extension tool.
Raid Lean

Measured Radiance

Power Word: Radiance applies Atonement for 2 additional sec, but its direct healing is slightly reduced. If cast on fewer than 3 injured allies, it refunds a small amount of mana.

Design reason:
Helps raid setup without making Radiance feel terrible in a small-party or boss-only dungeon moment.
Choice Node 4

Shield Prevention versus Release Throughput

This node changes whether the player prefers to soften damage before it lands or resolve it after it becomes Deferred Damage.

Prevention Lean

Tempered Shielding

Power Word: Shield absorbs more damage on allies with Atonement, but does not increase the Deferred Damage cap. When the shield breaks, the ally gains 1 Contrition.

Design reason:
Shielding feels valuable without returning to shield spam as the default answer to all incoming damage.
Release Lean

Penitent Release

Consuming Contrition increases the healing of the release by a small amount. Excess release healing reduces Deferred Damage on the same target instead of becoming full overheal.

Design reason:
Rewards clean release timing without making shields feel like a mistake.
Choice-node rule: Every option should preserve the same base loop: apply Atonement, build Contrition, smooth damage, and release pressure. The talent only changes where the player gains efficiency or comfort.
Scenario Examples

Concrete situations show how the same rules handle different pressure profiles.

Mythic+

5-target pull with tank spikes and group rot

  1. Apply Atonement to the tank with Power Word: Shield.
  2. Use Radiance as the group begins taking rot damage.
  3. Build Contrition through Mind Blast and offensive Penance.
  4. Tank spike lands. Part of the damage is deferred.
  5. Defensive Penance consumes tank Contrition to heal and clear Deferred Damage.
  6. Prayer of Healing or Radiance resolves the remaining group pressure.
Raid

Planned raid-wide burst event

  1. Prepare with Radiance and selected single-target applicators.
  2. Use Evangelism to extend Atonement and seed Contrition.
  3. Build additional Contrition through high-value offense.
  4. The burst hit lands. Atonement delays part of the damage.
  5. Radiance or Ultimate Penitence releases stored healing.
  6. Resume offense to rebuild Contrition and mana after the event.
Triage

One ally targeted by a dangerous mechanic

  1. Apply Atonement directly with Shield or Flash Heal.
  2. Build a few Contrition stacks if timing allows.
  3. Use Pain Suppression if the mechanic is lethal.
  4. Resolve the hit with defensive Penance.
  5. Use follow-up direct healing if Contrition setup was incomplete.
Sustained Rot

Constant party pressure over 20 seconds

  1. Keep Atonement rolling on likely damage targets.
  2. Use filler offense to maintain Contrition and mana.
  3. Use Prayer of Healing for small, efficient releases.
  4. Reserve Radiance for larger group spikes.
  5. Use Shield selectively when one ally’s deferred pool approaches cap.
Hero and Apex Talent Integration

Oracle and Voidweaver should modify the loop, not repair the base spec.

Oracle Direction

Evangelism

Evangelism extends Atonement and grants initial Contrition stacks. It accelerates preparation without creating a shield-dominant prevention model.

Voidweaver Direction

Entropic Rift

Entropic Rift increases Contrition generation and Siphoned Faith returns during offensive windows. It rewards aggressive healing without directly copying damage into healing.

Apex Cooldown

Ultimate Penitence

Ultimate Penitence acts as a major release and stabilization tool, healing allies while clearing Deferred Damage from high-pressure targets.

Voidweaver Payoff

Collapsing Void

Collapsing Void can serve as an offensive payoff that builds Contrition quickly, but it should not directly copy damage into healing.

Hero talent constraint: Oracle can emphasize preparation and stability. Voidweaver can emphasize offensive pressure and faster Contrition generation. Neither should be required to make the base Discipline loop functional.

UI Requirements

The system only works if the player can read ally state quickly.

Deferred Damage and Contrition cannot be hidden background systems. The player needs enough UI information to decide whether to build, release, shield, or use a cooldown.

Party and raid frame display

Deferred Damage should appear as a distinct health-frame overlay or segmented bar. The player should be able to see when an ally is safe, pressured, or approaching the deferred cap.

Contrition visibility

Contrition stacks should be visible on Atoned allies, ideally as a small stack count or compact icon. Release spells depend on the player knowing who is prepared.

Release feedback

When a release clears Deferred Damage, the UI should show a clear reduction and a readable combat effect. This makes the system feel responsive instead of abstract.

Cap warning

Allies near the Deferred Damage cap should receive a stronger visual state so the priest knows they need a release, shield, external cooldown, or direct healing immediately.

Tuning Notes

Numbers are placeholders. The system structure matters first.

Deferred Damage cap

Atonement delays 25% of incoming damage over 6 sec, capped at 25% of the target’s max health. This prevents infinite safety and scales naturally with gear.

Contrition cap

Contrition stacks up to 5 times per ally. Group releases should consume fewer stacks than single-target releases to preserve tuning control.

Shield cost

Shield stays expensive because it remains a premium mitigation tool. The redesign avoids making Shield the baseline maintenance spell.

Mana returns

Siphoned Faith should reward offensive uptime without making mana irrelevant. Returns should vary by spell and be tuned around dungeon and raid pacing.

Talent safety rails

Choice nodes should avoid hard target-count checks. A raid-leaning talent can scale better with more allies, but it should provide fallback value when only one or two allies are in danger.

Reactive healing floor

The system should reward preparation, but base direct healing must remain strong enough that Discipline can recover from unexpected targeting or imperfect setup.

What I Would Test

Validation should focus on clarity, pressure, and sustain.

Steady rot encounter

Confirm that Deferred Damage and Contrition releases answer rot without turning Discipline into Holy.

Large burst event

Test whether the cap prevents damage delay from trivializing planned raid mechanics.

High tank pressure

Validate Penance, Shield, and single-target Contrition release as the main tank-saving tools.

Single-target boss inside Mythic+

Ensure a player who took group or dungeon utility talents still has functional boss healing and does not feel punished for not swapping into a pure single-target build.

Mana stress dungeon

Confirm that offensive uptime stabilizes mana without making resource management irrelevant.

Talent cross-pressure test

Test every example choice node in its non-preferred situation: triage talents during rot, group talents during tank danger, raid talents in a dungeon, and Mythic+ talents on a boss.

Risks and Mitigations

Known risks should be solved by system rules, not emergency tuning.

Risk Why It Matters Mitigation
Deferred Damage feels invisible Players need to see delayed damage clearly to make informed decisions. Show a distinct deferred segment on party frames with cap visibility.
Contrition becomes mandatory for every heal Reactive healing should not feel unusable without setup. Base heals remain functional. Contrition improves efficiency and pressure resolution.
Siphoned Faith over-restores mana If mana becomes irrelevant, spell choice loses meaning. Use spell-specific returns and tune around real encounter uptime.
Shield loses identity Shield cannot dominate, but it still needs to feel important. Keep it expensive, strong, and situationally valuable for predictable danger.
Choice nodes become trap picks If a player feels useless whenever the encounter does not match the chosen talent, the tree is forcing content swaps instead of offering expression. Give each option fallback value outside its ideal scenario and keep core triage, group, rot, and burst answers baseline.
Deferred cap creates false safety Players may assume Atoned allies are safe while the deferred pool is already capped. Add cap warnings, overflow behavior, and clear UI treatment for allies approaching maximum deferred damage.

Expanded Tooltip Set

Sample in-game language for the redesigned kit.

Atonement
Your healing spells apply Atonement for 15 sec. Allies with Atonement have 25% of incoming damage delayed over 6 sec, up to 25% of their maximum health. Your offensive spells build Contrition on allies affected by Atonement.
Contrition
A stacking ally effect generated by your offensive spells while Atonement is active. Stacks up to 5 times for 12 sec. Penance, Power Word: Radiance, and Prayer of Healing consume Contrition to heal and reduce Deferred Damage.
Deferred Damage
Damage delayed by Atonement. Deferred Damage is dealt over time and cannot exceed 25% of the target’s maximum health. Healing releases can reduce or clear this pool.
Siphoned Faith
Your offensive spells restore mana when they generate Contrition. Smite restores a small amount of mana, while Mind Blast, Penance, and Shadow Word: Death restore increased amounts.
Penance
When cast on an ally, heals the target and consumes all Contrition on them, increasing healing and reducing Deferred Damage per stack consumed. When cast offensively, builds Contrition on Atoned allies.
Power Word: Radiance
Applies Atonement to nearby injured allies and consumes up to 3 Contrition from each affected ally, healing them and reducing their Deferred Damage.
Prayer of Healing
Heals injured allies and consumes 1 Contrition from each target healed. Efficient for sustained group pressure when full Radiance release is not required.
Power Word: Shield
Shields an ally and applies Atonement. High mana cost. Intended for predictable danger or emergency mitigation, not routine maintenance.
Evangelism
Extends active Atonements and grants 2 Contrition to affected allies. Used before planned damage to accelerate preparation.
Ultimate Penitence
Unleash a major penitence barrage, healing injured allies and consuming Contrition to reduce Deferred Damage on high-pressure targets.
Entropic Rift
Opens a Voidweaver pressure window. Offensive spells generate additional Contrition and restore increased mana through Siphoned Faith for the duration.
Void Shield
A premium shield upgrade used for predictable danger. Applies Atonement and strengthens immediate mitigation without becoming the default maintenance tool.
